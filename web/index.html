Crash Team Racing × Counter-Strike 1.6

CTR's own driving (the decompiled game, running in your browser) on Counter-Strike 1.6's maps, built from the game's own map files. Free drive for now: pick a map and drive it round.

This browser can't run the game: it needs WebAssembly JSPI, which Chrome and Edge have had since version 137. Open this page in one of those.

Free drive

No maps built yet: ./play.sh builds them from your Counter-Strike 1.6 install.

Split screen: every player past the first needs a gamepad (with one gamepad, it drives player 1 and the keyboard player 2).

A gamepad works too, with CTR's own buttons. Power slide: hold Right Shift through a turn and tap Left Shift when the exhaust turns black (either Shift starts the slide, the other one boosts); three boosts in a row and you're flying.

Crash Team Racing × de_dust2

CTR's own driving, AI and weapons (the decompiled game, running in your browser) on Counter-Strike's Dust 2: two race loops through long doors, long A, CT spawn, mid, B site and the tunnels, or the whole map to drive round.

This browser can't run the game: it needs WebAssembly JSPI, which Chrome and Edge have had since version 137. Open this page in one of those.

Mode

Split screen: every player past the first needs a gamepad (with one gamepad, it drives player 1 and the keyboard player 2). Three or four players race without the AI racers.

A gamepad works too, with CTR's own buttons. Power slide: hold Right Shift through a turn and tap Left Shift when the exhaust turns black (either Shift starts the slide, the other one boosts); three boosts in a row and you're flying.
